## Part 2: Value Framing, Trust & Economic Alignment Audit

### 1. The $997/mo Retainer Friction in `report/[domain]/page.tsx` (CRITICAL FLAW)
- **Problem:**
  On `/pricing` and the homepage, the core value proposition is emphatically:
  * "The $1,500 Complete Clinic Local Growth & AI Audit System"
  * "One-Time Investment • 0 Ongoing Monthly Retainers • No Software Traps"
  However, in `src/app/report/[domain]/page.tsx` (lines 55, 840–860):
  The Interactive ROI Modeler displays:
  * `Proposed GEO Retainer: $997/mo` (with toggle buttons $650, $997, $1250, $1500)
  * `Net Practice Profit: +$XX,XXX/mo (After retainer deduction)`
  * `Client Payback: 1st patient pays retainer`
- **Clinical Impact:**
  This immediately breaks trust. A doctor reading a public diagnostic report will believe NexusRank1 is just another monthly marketing agency trying to lock them into a $997/month subscription contract.
- **Remediation:**
  Refactor the ROI modeler in `src/app/report/[domain]/page.tsx` to align exactly with `PatientRoiCalculator.tsx`:
  - Show the **$1,500 One-Time System Investment** (0 ongoing retainers).
  - Calculate Net Annual Practice Profit: `(Annual Revenue Lift - $1,500)`.
  - Highlight the **1-Patient ROI Reality**: 1 single high-ticket implant case ($5,000–$25,000) completely pays for the entire $1,500 system 3x to 16x over.

### 2. High-Ticket Procedure Valuation Gap ($15,000 vs. $40,000)
- **Problem:**
  In `src/components/PatientRoiCalculator.tsx`:
  - The procedure value slider is capped at `$15,000` (`min="1000" max="15000" step="250"`).
  - Target users explicitly include surgical implantologists, cosmetic dentists, and medspa directors doing All-on-4, full-mouth reconstruction, and deep plane facelifts. Full-arch reconstruction is typically $25,000 to $40,000 (dual arch).
- **Remediation:**
  - Increase the slider upper bound to `$40,000` (`min="1000" max="40000" step="500"`).
  - Update slider tick labels: `$1,000 (Routine)` • `$15,000 (Single Arch)` • `$40,000 (Full-Mouth Reconstruction)`.
  - Update specialty sample procedures:
    - Dental: `Single Implant ($4,500), All-on-4 Full Arch ($24,000), Full-Mouth Restoration ($38,000)`
    - Plastic Surgery: `Rhinoplasty ($9,500), Deep Plane Facelift ($18,000), Mommy Makeover ($25,000)`

### 3. Missing Whop Checkout URL in `src/lib/config.ts`
- **Problem:**
  In `src/lib/config.ts`:
  `whopCheckoutUrl: process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_WHOP_CHECKOUT_URL || 'https://whop.com/checkout/nexusrank1-clinic-growth'`
  Acceptance criteria in `ORIGINAL_REQUEST.md` requires:
  `All external checkout links correctly point to https://whop.com/checkout/plan_e37e96b86e088/`
  Because no `.env` file exists in the repo, all Whop CTA buttons across the app currently fall back to `'https://whop.com/checkout/nexusrank1-clinic-growth'`.
- **Remediation:**
  Update default fallback in `src/lib/config.ts` to:
  `'https://whop.com/checkout/plan_e37e96b86e088/'`.

### 4. Chat Widget Fallback Coverage for Clinicians
- **Problem:**
  In `src/app/api/chat/route.ts`, if OpenRouter API is unavailable or unconfigured, the fallback response handles keywords like "wordpress", "pricing", and "pitch". However, if a doctor asks:
  - "A local competitor is taking my dental implant patients"
  - "Why is my clinic not showing on ChatGPT?"
  - "What is included in the $1,500 setup?"
  It drops through to a generic chatbot menu.
- **Remediation:**
  Add a dedicated Competitor Displacement & High-Ticket Acquisition handler in `buildFallbackChatResponse`:
  Highlighting how competitors win AI recommendations through structured CDT/CPT procedure nodes, how NexusRank1 reclaims the #1 recommendation, and the 90-day 1-patient ROI guarantee.

### 5. "Email Webmaster" vs. "Concierge Done-For-You Setup"
- **Problem:**
  In `src/components/InstantAuditBar.tsx` (lines 618, 1795):
  The toolbar offers an "Email Webmaster" button that outputs a generic template:
  `Please let us know when we can forward the code payload for deployment.`
  Clinicians hate dealing with code, and webmasters often ignore cryptic emails asking for "code payloads".
- **Remediation:**
  Elevate the **Zero-Work Concierge Setup** promise:
  "White-Glove 24-Hour Deployment Included: You never touch code. Our healthcare engineering team coordinates directly with your webmaster or installs directly onto your WordPress, Webflow, or custom site."
